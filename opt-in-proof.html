Luna Messaging Opt-In Process

Luna Messaging uses a public web signup flow for SMS opt-in. Users may submit the signup form with or without SMS consent. Only users who optionally check the SMS consent box are opted in to receive Luna Messaging text messages.

How users opt in

  1. The user visits the Luna Messaging signup page.
  2. The user enters their mobile phone number into the signup form.
  3. The user may optionally check an unchecked SMS consent checkbox to agree to receive utility, reminder, and assistant text messages from Luna Messaging.
  4. The SMS consent checkbox is not required to submit the signup form.
  5. The signup page includes links to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
  6. The signup page states that message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply.
  7. The submit button is functional and sends the user to a confirmation page after submission: signup-success.html.
  8. Only users who check the optional SMS consent box are opted in to receive Luna Messaging text messages.
  9. After submission, requests are manually reviewed before messaging is enabled.
  10. Users can reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out after enrollment.

Consent checkbox language

Optional SMS consent: By checking this box, I agree to receive utility, reminder, and assistant text messages from Luna Messaging at the mobile number I provided. Message frequency varies based on my interaction and requested reminders. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. I have read the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to submit this form.
